Process

How It Works

01

Capture

Record videos directly in the app or upload from your photos folder. Video-only — no photos allowed. Share local moments as they happen.

02

AI Understanding

Our multimodal AI processes every video — transcribing audio, analyzing visual scenes, detecting duplicates, and creating semantic embeddings. Videos become instantly searchable and discoverable.

03

Personal Discovery

Search via Ask the City, explore the Pulse Feed or Friends Feed, and get AI recommendations. The more you use CityPulse, the better it understands your preferences.

Why we're building CityPulse

Search & Discovery Beyond Keywords

Most apps treat your city like a database — stars, reviews, categories. CityPulse is different. We use multimodal AI to understand "cozy coffee shop" or "energetic block party" through vision and sound, delivering hyperlocal video recommendations that go far beyond keyword matching. Not 1,000 generic places, but the 3 that truly match your vibe.
